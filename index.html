About Us

More Than a Salon — A Place Where Beauty Begins.

For over 15 years, Shekhar Unisex Salon & Academy has been helping clients look and feel their best. Whether you're here for a fresh haircut, stunning hair colour, relaxing facial, bridal makeover, or professional grooming, our experienced team is dedicated to delivering outstanding results in a welcoming environment.

Beyond salon services, we proudly train aspiring beauty professionals through our academy, combining hands-on experience with industry-leading techniques to help students build successful careers.
